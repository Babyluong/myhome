## 1. Objective
Enable full In-Patient Pharmacy (Farmasi Pesakit Dalam / IPD) store location management within `StoreLocationManagementPage.tsx`. Users can switch departments between Logistik Farmasi (LOG) and Farmasi Pesakit Dalam (IPD), view standard KKM Inpatient physical storage locations (Oral, IV/Infusi, DDA, Chiller 2-8°C, Emergency Trolley), manage sub-locations (racks, shelves, compartments), and map items scoped to Inpatient Pharmacy facility inventory (`LOC-IPD-01`).

---

## 2. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Department Switcher**: A segmented control on `StoreLocationManagementPage.tsx` allows filtering by Department: `Logistik Farmasi (LOG)` and `Farmasi Pesakit Dalam (IPD)` (plus `Semua Jabatan`).
2. **Default IPD Locations**:
   - `IPD-ORAL-001`: Stor Ubat Oral (Pesakit Dalam) (Suhu Bilik)
   - `IPD-IV-001`: Stor Injeksi & Infusi IV (Suhu Bilik)
   - `IPD-DDA-001`: Peti Kawalan DDA & Psikotropik (Bilik Kawalan DDA)
   - `IPD-CHILL-001`: Peti Sejuk Farmasi Pesakit Dalam (2-8°C) (Peti Sejuk)
   - `IPD-EMG-001`: Troli Kecemasan & Resusitasi Wad (Suhu Bilik)
3. **Default Sub-Locations**:
   - Each IPD location is equipped with standard clinical sub-units (e.g. Rak Oral A & B with shelves, Rak Injeksi & Palet Infusi, Peti Berkunci DDA, Chiller Shelves, Crash Cart tiers).
4. **Scoped Facility Inventory**:
   - When viewing and assigning items in IPD locations, the inventory items and occupancy counts are scoped to Farmasi Pesakit Dalam (`LOC-IPD-01` / `2d1b7112-9c3a-4e2b-bb43-e8417e349a01`).
5. **Database Persistence**:
   - Store locations are saved to `store_locations` table in Supabase and cached locally.
6. **Depth & Token Standard**:
   - Adheres to modern depth tokens, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, 0 hardcoded styling flaws, and passes `npm run build` and `scripts/check-rules.cjs`.
