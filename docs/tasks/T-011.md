# T-011 — Medical Oxygen Dashboard Depth Design & Clinical Ergonomics Overhaul

**Goal:** Overhaul `OxygenDashboardPage.tsx` and `CylinderOfflineBanner.tsx` to meet the H.O.M.E. Modern Depth design standard (R5) and TasteSkill clinical density (7–8). Eliminate the "Carnival / Fruit Salad" neon color overload (purple, olive, lime green), standardize all 5 financial KPI cards on neutral clinical depth surfaces, fix severe WCAG 2.2 AA contrast failures (invisible dark-gray/black total costs), expand truncated Delivery Order codes, and introduce a collapsible toggle for the right-hand supplier fleet panel to maximize table scannability.

**User Decisions (2026-10-11 via Grill-Me Protocol):**
- Scope: Comprehensive Polish (Unify colors to H.O.M.E. Modern Depth, standardize all 5 KPI cards, compress header/telemetry stack, fix table contrast and column truncations).
- Right Drawer: Collapsible Drawer with toggle control allowing table expansion to 100% width.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Telemetry & Offline Banner Token Harmony (`CylinderOfflineBanner.tsx`)**:
   - Replace hardcoded `bg-white border-slate-200 text-slate-900` with dark-first design tokens (`bg-surface border-border-default text-text-secondary`).
   - Eliminate optical glare and white bar flash in dark mode.
2. **Unified Financial KPI Cards (`OxygenDashboardPage.tsx`)**:
   - Eliminate mismatched card backgrounds (olive `bg-emerald-50/40`, purple `bg-purple-50`, teal `bg-teal-50`).
   - Unify all 5 cards onto standard `--bg-surface` (`bg-surface border border-border-default shadow-elevation-1`).
   - All monetary values styled with `font-mono tabular-nums text-text-primary` with crisp legibility.
   - Replace arbitrary color badge fills with clean tokenized icons and subtle status pills.
3. **Table Scannability & WCAG 2.2 AA Contrast Gate**:
   - Fix Total Cost column text: replace invisible `text-slate-950` with high-contrast `text-text-primary font-mono tabular-nums`.
   - Remove low-contrast `text-purple-700` and `text-teal-600` on DO and loan amounts; adopt readable semantic tokens.
   - Expand Delivery Order container to eliminate awkward ellipsis truncation (`EDK/90-0...`).
   - Soften status badges to translucent chips (`bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-400 border border-emerald-500/20`).
4. **Collapsible Supplier Fleet Sidebar**:
   - Add stateful toggle (`isRightSidebarOpen`) in table header.
   - When collapsed, delivery ledger table expands smoothly to full viewport width.
   - Restyle right widgets (Active Cylinder Prices & Government Warrant status) to remove hardcoded `bg-emerald-50`, `border-emerald-200`, and `bg-purple-50`.
5. **Code & Rule Gate**:
   - Zero hardcoded hex values added.
   - Script-First transform executed deterministically.
   - `scripts/check-rules.cjs` PASS, `npm run build` PASS.
