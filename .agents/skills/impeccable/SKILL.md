# UI critique and polish

This is a concise local adaptation of Impeccable-inspired design critique, not a complete upstream pack. Root AGENTS.md, docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md and the user's approved brief govern the result.

- Identify page purpose first. Operational workflows favor legible data, predictable navigation and compact actions. Login/hub identity surfaces may use approved display typography, intentional depth, gradients and a visible 3D hero.
- Use product tokens for colors, spacing, radii, typography and elevation. Resting surfaces and interactive shadows are allowed where the design system defines them; do not substitute a universal shadow ban.
- Group by meaning. Avoid redundant nested panels and visual effects without a role in the approved composition. A deliberate login composition need not resemble a data table.
- Keep important text, credential inputs and primary actions clear. Check actual contrast, keyboard focus, responsive fit and empty/loading/error feedback where applicable.
- Style states the control actually has. A labeled accessible spinner is valid for a pending action; skeletons fit content loading. Do not invent disabled or loading states merely to satisfy a fixed checklist.
- Use tabular numerals for comparable operational values and coherent typography for the page. An approved identity hero can use a display scale distinct from module body text.
- Critique actual rendered evidence in both themes and agreed viewports. A source review or successful build cannot establish visual acceptance.

Report concrete observations, their effect and the responsible owner to Amri. Do not claim production readiness or a full upstream-rule count from this local checklist.
