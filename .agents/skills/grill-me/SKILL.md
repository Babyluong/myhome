# Grill decisions

Babybun owns the interview. Find relevant facts in the repository instead of asking the user for them.

Map unresolved decisions and their dependencies. Ask all independent questions whose prerequisites are settled in one round, with a clear recommendation; defer dependent questions until their prerequisite is answered. Stop when the user's desired behavior, scope, and meaningful risks are resolved. Do not ask questions on axes irrelevant to the task. Fold answers into Babybun's plan; wait for its approval gate before implementation.
